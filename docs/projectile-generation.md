# Proyectiles de la ampliación de personajes

Generados con la herramienta integrada imagegen; fuente inmutable guardada en `Assets/art/effects/roster/projectiles-source-v1.png`. Aunque se solicitó magenta, la salida seleccionada ya contiene transparencia real. El importador conserva esa transparencia sin quitar los tonos violetas. Catálogo: `Assets/Resources/MonsterPouch/ProjectileArt.asset`.

32 cuadros: 4 de vuelo y 4 de impacto para cada uno de Anuik, Tauris, Stein y Bugui. El efecto de impacto se dispara con el evento de daño de la simulación; el mordisco mortal tiene una explosión mayor. Los efectos respetan pausa y cambio de partida.

No grid lines, no text, no labels, no character, no scenery. Crisp squared pixel clusters and hard edges, no blurry gradients, no anti-aliasing, no castshadow. Small jewel-like videogame effects with bold dark outlines. Each sprite centered in its individual cell with at least 20px empty margins. Row1: pink/red HEART projectiles: columns1-4 four flight pulsation frames of one cute heart, pointing right with small pink speed streak; cols5-8 heart impact bursting into small pink heart fragments, expansion then dissipation. Row2: THROWN BITE projectiles, cols1-4 four frames of a spectral ivory upper/lower jaw with triangular teeth, snapping jaws and coralpink energy, orientedright; cols5-8 large powerful bite slam snapping shut then toothshaped impact shards. Row3: electric cyan/yellow LIGHTNING projectiles, cols1-4 crackling rightward zigzag bolt varied frames, cols5-8 electric impact star with angular branches then dissipating sparks. Row4: BUGUI violet/blue magical energy projectiles, cols1-4 animated compact faceted star-orb with purple core and brightblue flame trail, cols5-8 blooming violet explosion with pixel sparks and fading particles. All sprites with generous margins, no neighboring cell overlap. Beautiful cohesive retro tactical-autobattler style. Solid magenta ONLY background, don't put magenta in sprites.
